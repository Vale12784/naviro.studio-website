Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

NAVIRO Studio ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

1. Who We Are

NAVIRO Studio is an independent digital design and development studio. Our designated Information Officer is the founder of NAVIRO Studio, who can be contacted at hello@naviro.co.za.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Information you provide: When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and details about your project.
  • Analytics and Tracking Data: We use a tool called PostHog to understand how visitors interact with our website. This may include your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and buttons clicked.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide quotes for our services.
  • To analyse website traffic and improve the user experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Cookies and Third Parties

We do not sell your data. However, we use trusted third-party services to operate our website:

  • Formspree: Handles the secure delivery of contact form submissions to our email.
  • PostHog: Processes analytics data securely to help us understand website usage. We configure PostHog to respect your browser's privacy settings.

5. Your Rights (POPIA)

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request that we correct or delete your personal information.
  • Object to the processing of your personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at hello@naviro.co.za.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to secure your data. Our website is served over a secure HTTPS connection, and third-party providers (like PostHog and Formspree) use industry-standard encryption.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
